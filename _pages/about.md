I am an undergraduate student in Electrical Engineering at Purdue University,
with interests in power systems, grid planning, and large-load integration.

I am currently working with Prof. Junjie Qin on the NextGW project,
focusing on bus-level load modeling and learning-based methods for
grid hosting-capacity analysis.

Previously, I conducted summer research with Prof. Kaiyu Fu at the
University of Notre Dame, contributing to measurement hardware and
PCB design for a multichannel potentiostat platform.

I am seeking PhD opportunities in power and energy systems for Fall 2027.
